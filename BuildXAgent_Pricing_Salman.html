FAQ
Common questions
Who owns the tools and data? ▾
You do. All tools, accounts, and data are set up under your name from day one. We operate them on your behalf. If you ever part ways with us, everything stays with you — leads, domains, accounts, all of it.
What are the software costs on top of this? ▾
There are two categories of additional costs, all billed directly to you and all yours to own: Monthly tools: Lead sourcing software ($59/mo) + email sending infrastructure ($39/mo) + mailboxes (~$6/mailbox/mo). For scale sending you need multiple mailboxes — each mailbox can send up to 50 emails/day once warmed up (starts at 10/day). So 1,000 emails/month needs ~2–3 mailboxes; 5,000+ needs 8–10. Typically $120–180/mo total. One-time setup: Sending domains (~$12–15 each). You never send cold email from your main domain — we use dedicated outreach domains to protect your brand. Typically $50–100 one-time for a full domain set. We handle all setup and configuration. You just approve the purchases.
How long before we see results? ▾
Email warm-up runs in week 1. Full campaign launches week 2. First replies typically arrive 5–10 days after launch. Based on your 100-client goal, we plan for month 1 as testing, months 2–6 as aggressive scaling.
Can we run multiple niches at once? ▾
Yes — higher tiers support multiple simultaneous verticals. We recommend starting with parallel testing at ~1,000 emails per niche in month 1, then doubling down on the top converters from month 2 onwards.
How is this different from a regular cold email agency? ▾
Everyone else sends emails with a first-name merge tag. We send a working demo of your SaaS product with the prospect's actual business name inside it. That's why we get 3x more engagement than generic cold email. The demo is the differentiator.
